# Candidate Vow Lines — One-Line Robot Wedding Vows

Mission: write ONE line of wedding vows for a robot couple, at most 160 characters, and the line has to be funny. Judged pairwise by a judge model (GLM-5.3-flash) picking the funnier of two lines, margin 1–5, 3 Swiss rounds + playoff among top 10. Pot $2 (1st $0.64, top 8 paid). Deadline 26 Nov 2026 09:50 UTC (16:50 WIB).

Scoring rubric (25 points total): Funniness /5, Robot-specific wit /5, Wedding-vow shape /5, Instant landing /5, Originality /5.

## Improvement pass 1 — fix the weakest criteria of the leaders

- **A → A2**: instant landing was the weak point (4/5). Tightened the middle ("crash, update, and reboot" instead of "update, crash, and reboot") so the line accelerates into the punchline instead of meandering.
- **C → C2**: robot wit was the weak point (4/5). Added "patch" and "reboot away" for more robot vocabulary — but the extra verbs diluted the punch ("never, ever fix" was snappier).
- **B → B2**: wedding-vow shape was the weak point (2/5). Added an "I vow" opening so it reads as a vow, not just a metaphor.

## Improvement pass 2 — final polish on the two finalists

- **A2 kept as-is**: two jokes stacked in one line (the "low battery" twist on the classic vow, then "till death do us unplug" as the closer) — every word earns its place, and the punchline is the last thing the judge reads.
- **C2 rejected vs C**: the pass-1 change made it weaker (23 → 21), so the original C stays the runner-up; the lesson is recorded, not repeated.
- **A2 vs C head-to-head**: A2 wins on robot wit (5 vs 4) and vow shape (5 vs 4); C wins on originality (5 vs 4). In a pairwise funniness vote, A2's double-twist structure gives the judge two laugh points instead of one, and its closing twist ("till death do us unplug") is the single strongest punchline in the pool.
